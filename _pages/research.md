My research interests lie at the intersection of **non-equilibrium statistical physics, complex systems, stochastic dynamics, and computational physics**.

I am particularly interested in how fluctuations, rare events, and non-equilibrium driving shape the behavior of dynamical systems.

---

## Current Research

### Rare events and active systems

I am currently carrying out my Master's thesis research at the **Institute for Theoretical Physics** and the **Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization** in Göttingen.

I work in **Prof. Peter Sollich's group**, in collaboration with **Johannes Zierenberg**.

My project focuses on:

* **Barrier crossing**
* **Rare-event sampling**
* **Symmetry breaking**
* **Active Brownian particles**
* **Non-equilibrium dynamics**

More specifically, I study barrier crossing and symmetry breaking in a **double-well potential driven by an Active Brownian Particle**.

The broader goal of this work is to understand how non-equilibrium activity modifies transition dynamics and rare-event behavior.

## Complex Systems and Epidemiological Modeling

### Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization

As a research assistant in **Prof. Viola Priesemann's Complex Systems Theory group**, I worked on mathematical models describing interacting pathogens.

I developed and analyzed a **multi-pathogen compartmental model** using:

* Differential equations
* Linear stability analysis
* Computational modeling
* Numerical analysis

This work contributed to research published in ***Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*** and ***Chaos, Solitons & Fractals***.

## Bachelor's Thesis

### Trajectory Statistics and Current Fluctuations in Non-Equilibrium Systems

**University of Granada, 2025**

My Bachelor's thesis focused on the statistics of trajectories and current fluctuations in systems away from equilibrium.

This project introduced me to the study of fluctuating trajectories and non-equilibrium statistical physics, and motivated my continued work on stochastic dynamics and rare events.
