# Hi, I'm Antonio 👋

I am a **physicist** interested in understanding how complex
systems behave **far from equilibrium**.

My work combines **statistical physics, computational modeling,
stochastic dynamics, and numerical methods**, with a particular focus on
**rare events, active matter, and complex systems**.

I am currently pursuing an **MSc in Complex Systems and Biophysics at
Georg-August-Universität Göttingen** and carrying out my Master's research
at the **Institute for Theoretical Physics** and the
**Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization**.

## About me

My research interests lie at the intersection of **non-equilibrium
statistical physics, complex systems, and biophysics**.

I am particularly interested in how fluctuations, stochasticity, and
collective dynamics determine the behavior of systems away from
equilibrium.

During my studies, I have worked on problems ranging from
**trajectory statistics and current fluctuations** to
**multi-pathogen dynamical models**, and I am currently studying
**barrier-crossing phenomena and rare events in active systems**.

## 🔬 Current research

### Rare events in active systems

My current Master's thesis research focuses on **barrier crossing and
rare-event sampling** in a double-well potential
driven by an **Active Brownian Particle**.

I carry out this work in **Prof. Peter Sollich's group** at the Institute
for Theoretical Physics in Göttingen, in collaboration with
**Johannes Zierenberg** at the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and
Self-Organization.

The broader motivation is to understand how noise
changes transition pathways, fluctuations, and rare-event statistics.
